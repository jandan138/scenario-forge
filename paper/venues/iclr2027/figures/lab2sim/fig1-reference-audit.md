# Lab2Sim Figure 1 reference and asset audit

Date: 2026-09-17

## Communication job

Text, an image, or a wet-lab demonstration enters one Lab2Sim agent and leaves
as diverse robot-ready Isaac laboratory scenes.

## Reference-led decisions

- Genesis-LLM ACL 2027 K1: adopt the three-region editorial hierarchy and
  four-frame evidence rhythm; reject its task-specific branch graph.
- Genesis-LLM contract teaser: show a concrete frozen-spec payload instead of a
  generic document icon; reject its dense gate annotations.
- VIMA and PaLM-E: combine the modalities into one compact request region.
- GenSim and RoboGen: spend the terminal visual area on a diverse output
  gallery rather than on architecture decoration.

This is an inspired composition. No source figure, icon arrangement, labels, or
scientific graph is copied.

## Baseline gaps

1. Three separate input cards consume too much width.
2. Their arrows stop at the agent boundary instead of reaching the first stage.
3. A large pale container surrounds four small, visually identical cards.
4. The frozen asset spec and ConvertAsset boundary are represented generically.
5. One low-contrast oven crop cannot show output diversity.

## Final-output selection

Only task heads marked `current_delivery` are presented as final products:

- Fehling reducing-sugar water bath r10;
- task09 powder bottle r4;
- traditional acid-base titration r1.7;
- task12 OVEN 125 unload with dual glassware r2.

The centrifuge candidate, powder r6.0 experiments, and unrelated Lift2
prototype views are excluded from the Figure 1 output strip. Camera-only
retakes do not alter scene USD or task state and do not add a robot-policy
claim.

The asset review changed the initial retake estimate. Fehling r10 lacked a
usable full-station state and received a new opt-in `hero_tabletop` camera.
Powder r4, titration r1.7, and OVEN r2 already had current-delivery evidence
with the required content. They were retained after visible-only review:
powder receives a HUD crop, titration is copied, and OVEN receives a documented
global brightness lift. No scene state or geometry was changed.
