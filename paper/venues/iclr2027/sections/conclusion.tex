\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A protocol and a USD scene used to be different documents written by
different people. Lab2Sim is an agent that compiles one into the other: it
searches out the instrument a protocol names, writes the specification that
fixes what a robot must be able to grasp, open, and place, generates the
articulated USD, and lets ConvertAsset decide whether the result may enter
the laboratory. The stations in this paper are the compiler's output, and the
two traces in Figure~\ref{fig:real-spec} are the compiler's audit trail. The
next step is the obvious one: put the Lift2 to work in them.
