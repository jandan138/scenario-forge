\begin{abstract}
A wet-lab protocol names the instruments a robot must touch. A physics
simulator needs USD: articulated joints, colliders, materials, and a floor
the robot can stand on. Lab2Sim is an agent that compiles the first document
into the second. Given a text instruction, a catalog photograph, or a
demonstration video, the agent searches out the real instrument, writes an
asset specification that fixes its task-critical geometry and articulation,
iterates articulated USD with a Blender skill, and admits the result through
ConvertAsset into an Isaac scene a Lift2 can enter. We show the laboratories
the agent produces---Fehling baths, titration stands, powder weighing, a
drying oven, and stopper, glass-rod, stir-bar, and water-bath stations---and
trace two instruments, the IKA OVEN~125 and a traditional burette, from
manufacturer evidence through the agent-written specification to the admitted
USD.
\end{abstract}
