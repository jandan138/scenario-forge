# Fehling r9: front-on in-bath color through the beaker

## Decision

r8 OmniGlass water still failed the user's actual observation: looking
straight through the beaker wall and fake bath at the immersed column,
especially brick-red at the round bottom. r8 had no in-bath video. Its
`through_wall` camera grazed the waterline; its closeups looked down at color
above the waterline. Those are not the acceptance views.

r9 is a new package from frozen r8. Fill `0.8`, `water:profile_m`, tube, 8 mL
five-layer reaction and physics stay unchanged. Visual water `body` is a
~1.5 mm lining with an open core; the meniscus is a ring, not a full OmniGlass
disk (a full disk sat on the line of sight to the tip).

## Acceptance

`front_bath`: eye-level, ~30 cm on −Y, looking at the lower sample. Video is a
keyframe replay of immersed `t3…t30` from that camera; the tube stays in the
bath. Withdrawn crops are not the gate.
