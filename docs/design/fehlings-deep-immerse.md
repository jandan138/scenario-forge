# Fehling r10: deep upright immerse, same fill

## Decision

r9 looked through the beaker from the front, but heating playback only dipped
the tube about 6 mm below the waterline (55° then upright). Most of the 8 mL
column sat in air. The user asked to keep `fill_height_ratio=0.8` and lower the
tube until the sample is under the fake water.

r10 copies frozen r9 water (lining + meniscus ring, OmniGlass, fill 0.8), tube,
and five-layer controller. Color replay is upright, centered, with the tube
origin about 1.5 mm above the visual-water floor. `tilted_contact` stays a
contact test, not a color camera.

A 150 mm tube cannot go fully under 87 mm of water in this beaker. Success is
the 8 mL column below the waterline; the mouth still sticks out.

## Acceptance

`front_bath` looks at the midpoint of the submerged tube. Video is in-bath
keyframes with that pose. Withdrawn crops are not the gate.
