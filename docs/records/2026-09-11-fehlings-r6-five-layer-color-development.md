## Implementation

- Qualified r5 source; no physics, glass, bath or placement changes.
- Original sample z=4–32.608117 mm, five layers each 5.721623 mm high.
  Equal heights are not equal volumes in the tapered tube.
- Five exterior Mesh segments with matching rings/normals; only the global
  bottom and top are capped. No internal disks or runtime geometry/visibility writes.
- Five UsdPreviewSurface materials, explicit intermediate keyframes and three
  changing inputs each. The operator sees colors evolve continuously and at
  different rates along the column; at 30 s all shader values converge.
- Ordered layer relations/telemetry replace the r5 sample/sediment targets and
  fixed sediment-height values. Policy is `visual_five_layers_v6`.
- Source compute and contact functions retained, observation and success latch
  retained, heating gate changed to30 s. Task/config/portable metric IDs updated.
- Intermediate withdrawn images record actual prescribed poses and paused
  material states; they are not fake out-of-water render positions for in-water states.

## Limits

Through retained glass/lighting, the lower taper is darker, yellow can be ochre
and orange-red can be red-brown. Intermediate height-dependent differences are
visible in withdrawn closeups; water/rim occlusion reduces their readability
while immersed. Layer boundaries remain discrete, without real cloudy flow,
particle motion or mixing. The new appearance follows the reference's broad
sequence and endpoint, not pixel-level video reproduction. Fake liquid follows
the tube rigidly. No spilling, true chemistry, thermal solution, robot grasp
success or Isaac Sim 4.1 qualification is claimed.
